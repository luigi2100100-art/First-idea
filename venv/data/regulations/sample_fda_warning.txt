FDA Regulation Rule: Dietary supplements cannot claim to diagnose, mitigate, treat, cure, or prevent any disease (201 U.S.C. 321(g)(1)). Structure/function claims are permitted only if they describe the role of a nutrient or dietary ingredient intended to affect the structure or function in humans, and must bear the mandatory disclaimers. Explicit disease claims like "cures joint inflammation" or "lowers blood pressure" trigger immediate regulatory warning letters and FTC enforcement actions.
